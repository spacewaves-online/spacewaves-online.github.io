    Space Waves 

    Space Waves is an arcade-style game where you guide an arrow through a path filled with obstacles, aiming to reach the end of each level without crashing. The controls are simple, but staying on course becomes harder as the pace increases.

    The game includes 33 levels, and you’re free to pick any stage at any time instead of progressing in a fixed order. Each level comes with its own difficulty rating, shown using face icons, so you can quickly judge how challenging it will be before starting.

    How to Play Space Waves

    What Is Your Goal?

    Your objective is to guide the arrow safely through each level while avoiding collisions. You need to dodge obstacles, take down enemy ships, and collect power-ups to boost your score and stay alive. Every level follows the same core goal: reach the end without hitting walls, spikes, or rotating hazards.

    Endless Obstacles

    Each run places you inside space tunnels filled with hazards. These paths are designed to keep you alert, with obstacles like walls, spikes, and spinning gears appearing along the way. You must control your arrow carefully to avoid crashing into them.

    Difficulty Levels

    Levels are marked by colors and vary in complexity. Easier stages provide wider paths and smoother angles, making movement more forgiving. As difficulty increases, tunnels become tighter and obstacles appear more frequently, requiring faster reactions and better control. You can choose any level at any time, including the most challenging ones.

    Enemy Spaceships

    Enemy ships appear during gameplay and attack as you progress. You can destroy them to gain points and unlock power-ups. These upgrades can improve your abilities, such as boosting firepower, granting temporary protection, or slowing down the pace to make navigation easier.

    Controls

    PC:

    • Left Mouse Button / W / Spacebar – Hold to move up
    • Release Key/Button – Move down

    Mobile:

    • Tap and Hold Screen – Move up
    • Release – Move down

    Shooting:

    • Tap Screen / Click – Fire laser

    Movement is based on holding and releasing rather than directional keys, so timing your input is key to avoiding obstacles and staying in control.

    Game Modes

    World Mode 1

    World Mode 1 is the starting stage and is designed with lower difficulty. It features wider paths and fewer obstacles, making it easier to understand the controls and basic mechanics. This mode is ideal for learning how to guide the arrow accurately before moving on to harder levels.

    Endless Mode

    Endless Mode has no finish point. The goal is to survive as long as possible while obstacles continue to appear. The pace increases over time, forcing you to react quickly and stay focused. It’s a good way to test your consistency and push for higher scores.

    Both modes serve different purposes. World Mode 1 helps you build control and confidence, while Endless Mode focuses on endurance and reaction speed. Trying both will give you a better understanding of the game and improve overall performance.

    Tips to Play Space Waves

    • Practice consistently – The more you play, the better you’ll get at controlling the arrow and reacting to obstacles.
    • Use power-ups carefully – Don’t waste them. Save them for tight sections or heavy obstacle patterns where they make a real difference.
    • Ignore distractions – Background visuals and effects can throw you off. Keep your focus on obstacles and enemy ships.
    • Control your movement – Use small zigzag motions to adjust position while maintaining forward flow, especially in narrow paths.
    • Upgrade wisely – Improve stats like speed, firepower, and power-up duration to handle later levels more effectively.
